Tamil Song Finder பாடல் தேடல்

Find a song from what you remember: the movie, actors, music director, lyricist, singers, director, or the year. Fill in any combination of fields.

Separate several names with commas to require all of them (e.g. singers SPB, Janaki). Spelling variants work: Ilayaraja finds Ilaiyaraaja. Common nicknames such as SPB, ARR, MSV, TMS, Raja work too.
